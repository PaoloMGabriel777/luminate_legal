## 1. Overview

Luminate ("we," "our," or "the app") is a mental health journaling app for iOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device
Luminate stores the following data exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework:

- Journal entries (text, mood ratings, stress levels, emotions, photos)
- User profile and preferences
- Progress data (XP, levels, achievements, streaks)
- Monthly summaries

**This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the AI conversation feature (see Section 2.2).**

### 2.2 Data Sent to Third-Party AI Services
When you use the AI Conversation feature, the text content of your journal entry or message is sent to **OpenAI** (openai.com) to generate a response. By using this feature, you consent to that data being processed by OpenAI under their [Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy).

- We do not store your AI conversation data on any server.
- We do not link AI conversation content to any persistent identifier.
- You can choose not to use the AI Conversation feature to keep all data fully local.

### 2.3 Purchase and Subscription Data
Luminate uses **RevenueCat** and **Superwall** to manage subscriptions (Luminate Pro). These services may collect:

- A pseudonymous device/user identifier
- Purchase history and subscription status
- Transaction metadata from the App Store

This data is processed by RevenueCat and Superwall under their respective privacy policies. We do not have access to your payment details — all transactions are handled by Apple.

### 2.4 Photos
If you use the photo journaling feature, photos are taken via your device camera and stored locally in the app's SwiftData store. Photos are not uploaded to any server.

### 2.5 Crash and Diagnostic Data
We may receive anonymized crash reports through Apple's built-in crash reporting, subject to your device's analytics sharing settings. This does not include journal content.

---

## 3. Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not create user accounts.
- We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information.
- We do not use advertising SDKs or sell your data to third parties.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Luminate is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us at Thepaologabriel@yahoo.com.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

All journal data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all app data at any time by:

1. Deleting individual entries within the app.
2. Uninstalling Luminate from your device, which permanently removes all locally stored data.

For subscription-related data held by RevenueCat, please refer to [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

---

## 6. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your data. Since journal data is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData, it is protected by your device's built-in encryption and passcode/biometric security. We strongly recommend enabling Face ID or a device passcode.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access your personal data
- Delete your personal data
- Opt out of data processing

Since Luminate stores data locally on your device, you have direct control over your data at all times. For any concerns about third-party data (OpenAI, RevenueCat, Superwall), please contact those services directly or reach us at Thepaologabriel@yahoo.com.
